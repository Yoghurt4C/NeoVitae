# 1.1.45

## Changes

- Added the `neovitae:nexus_loot_blacklist` item tag. Items in it never drop from the Torment Nexus or Deus Torment Nexus.
- Added the Unbounded Flight upgrade, which lets fully evolved Sentient Armor fly.
- Added Revenant Plating and the Deus Shard.
- The Lex Vitae beam now mines at your mining speed, so Efficiency, Haste and the Spiritus bonus all make it faster.
- Area mining with the Lex Vitae costs far less Spiritus: one point per hit, plus one for every five extra blocks.
- The Lex Vitae now outlines the blocks it is about to break, and blocks crack visibly under the beam before they break.
- Chopping a tree with the Lex Vitae now fells the whole tree. Sneak to take a single log.
- The Lex Vitae now gains Growth Levels as it mines and fights. Each level adds Mining Efficiency and lets it shrug off more wear, and every fifteenth level adds Fortune. Hold Shift over it to see its growth.

## Fixes

- The Ritual Anchor now resists explosions as well as ritual stones do (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#203).
- The Lex Vitae beam no longer charges twice for the block it hits.
- The guide book now describes the Ritual Configurator, Crystallum Fractura and Spirit Accumulator correctly.
- The Keep tooltip on the Output Routing Node's Spiritus tab now describes what it does.
- The fluid filter tooltips on routing nodes now describe the controls correctly.
- The Demon Lantern's guide book page no longer runs off the bottom; it is now split across two pages.
- The Elytra Upgrade Tome can now be crafted from a Binding Reagent and an Elytra in an Alchemy Array.

## Translations

- Updated Simplified Chinese translation, thanks to ChuijkYahus (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#210).
- Corrected Simplified Chinese text on the routing node screens and the Crystallum Fractura Fortune page.
