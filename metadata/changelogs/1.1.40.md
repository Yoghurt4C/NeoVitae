# 1.1.40

## Changes

- Addons can listen for the new `NeoVitaeCraftedEvent.AlchemyTable` event to change what a Tabula Vitae craft makes, the same way the `Altar` and `Forge` events work. It also fires when the Sanguine Crafter runs a Tabula Vitae recipe.
- The Sanguine Crafter no longer needs Spiritus Ruina or Spiritus Invictus shards to run Tabula Vitae or Hellfire Forge recipes. It pays the same costs as the table and the forge, plus its usual EV per craft. Shards left in an existing crafter move to its output buffer.
- Explosive Powder and the Reinforced, Hellforged and Deus Explosive Cells are gone from the game. Any still in a world or inventory disappear when the world loads.

## Fixes

- Killing a large Slime of Vitae no longer splits it into twice as many smaller slimes as it should.
