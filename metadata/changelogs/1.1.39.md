# 1.1.39

## Changes

- New Sanguine Crafter: a two-block-high 3x3 multiblock that crafts crafting table, Tabula Vitae and Hellfire Forge recipes from an input buffer you can pipe into or automate with RS/Ae2. Upgrade casings let it craft several items at once or work faster, and it keeps its chunks loaded while its owner is online (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#166, breakinblocks/NeoVitae#168).
- The Rhythm of the Beating Anvil ritual is removed; the Sanguine Crafter replaces it. A Master Ritual Stone that was running it switches off when loaded, and its runes can be rebuilt as another ritual (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#191).
- The Tabula Vitae and Hellfire Forge can push finished items out of a side you choose. Select the output slot and right-click a side button.
- The Blood Tank, the Blood Tank upgrade and the Orb Filling Link accept any colorless glass, so clear glass from other mods works in place of vanilla glass.
- New Soul Snare flask effect, made at the Tabula Vitae from a Simple Catalyst and a Petty Spiritus Gem. Throw it as a splash or lingering flask to snare a group of monsters, then kill them with any weapon to collect Spiritus Essence (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#185).
- The Hellfire Forge has the same side configuration as the Tabula Vitae. Click an empty slot, gem slot included, to choose which sides hoppers and pipes can use it from. Existing forges keep their current sides (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#192).
- Snared monsters now only drop Spiritus Essence when a player kills them or hit them shortly before they died. Monsters killed by traps or mob farms on their own drop nothing.

## Fixes

- Neo Vitae blocks now show up on maps, including Xaero's Minimap and World Map and vanilla map items. Before, most of them were drawn as if nothing was there.
- The Orb Tiers page in the Scriptura Vitae no longer runs off the page. It shrinks to fit when addons add more orbs (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#186).
- The Tower Guards at the top of the dungeon's spiral staircase room show their name again instead of raw text (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#184).
- Right-clicking a Neo Vitae machine such as the Hellfire Forge, Tabula Vitae, Athanor, Teleposer or a routing node while in spectator mode no longer disconnects you.
- The Void Sigil now removes fluid when there is a block behind it within reach. Before, nothing happened (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#193).
