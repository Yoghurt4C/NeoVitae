# 1.1.44

## Changes

- Blood that arrives faster than the Ara Vitae's basin can hold now waits in an overflow reserve of up to 20 times the basin's capacity, instead of being lost. The Seer's Sigil and Jade show the reserve.
- The Divinus Orb of Vitae now charges much faster in an Orb Vitae Link or on the altar (fill rate 50 to 160).
