# 1.1.43

## Additions

- CC: Tweaked computers and turtles can read the Ara Vitae: its EV, capacity, tier and crafting progress (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#201).
- CC: Tweaked can also read the Vitae Link, Orb Vitae Link, Master Ritual Stone, Spirit Accumulator, Athanor, Tabula Vitae, Hellfire Forge and Sanguine Crafter.
- The Endless Quarry reaches further: a Hellforged Block foundation gives radius 127, and the new Sanguine Deus Block gives radius 255 (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#198).
- New Sanguine Deus Block, crafted in the Athanor from a Hellforged Block with a Divinus Orb, 10,000 mB of Essentia Vitae and 40 of every Spiritus. It works as a beacon base.

## Fixes

- Fierce Strike level 5 cost fewer upgrade points than level 4. It now costs 49.
- Sentient Armor upgrade pages in Scriptura Vitae ran off the bottom of the page. The training and maximum level details now have a page of their own (breakinblocks/NeoVitae#200).
- Tabula Vitae, Hellfire Forge and flask recipes in Scriptura Vitae sat too far right, so the output slot overlapped the edge of the page.
