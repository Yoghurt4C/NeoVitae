# 1.1.42

## Fixes

- Blood Siphon showed a blood stream and played its sound on every hit, which caused heavy lag with fast or multi-hit weapons. The stream and sound now show at most twice a second per player and are only sent to players within 32 blocks. EV is still gained on every hit.
- Fierce Strike level 5 cost fewer upgrade points than level 4. It now costs 49.
