# 1.1.46

## Fixes

- The Sigil of Bound Treasures no longer disconnects you when the bound container is out of view. Most containers still open from anywhere; storage from mods that need the block close by now shows a message instead.
- Recipes that need several of the same item, such as the Prismatic Spiritus Gem and Deus Shard, now craft in the Athanor when those items share one slot, so AE2 and other automation can feed it.
